Kolibant Packing · e-commerce boxes · Izmir

Enter your box size, see the shipping weight

Kolibant cuts boxes to fit e-commerce products, packs them and hands them to a contracted courier. Length × width × height ÷ 3000 = volumetric weight; the greater of volumetric weight and kilograms is charged. The table shows an estimated shipping price by zone. Images are representative; the brand, tariff and content are fictional.

Packing line

Six stations from order to courier

Every order moves in this order; times are for a typical single-item parcel. Images are representative; the brand, tariff and content are fictional.

  1. 01straighten

    Station 1

    Measure and weigh

    The product’s three dimensions and weight are read.

    schedule20 s

  2. 02content_cut

    Station 2

    Box cutting

    Corrugated board is cut to the product, leaving no gap.

    schedule40 s

  3. 03print

    Station 3

    Logo and return label

    A one-colour logo and return label are printed.

    schedule15 s

  4. 04eco

    Station 4

    Paper fill

    Fragile items are held with paper fill; no plastic.

    schedule30 s

  5. 05inventory_2

    Taping

    Sealed with paper tape and weighed again.
